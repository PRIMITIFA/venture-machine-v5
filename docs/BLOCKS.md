# Blocks 01-18: inputs, outputs, tests

Each block has a verdict:
- **pass**: continue.
- **warn**: continue, and the warning is noted.
- **fail**: the block missed its own bar (for example fewer than 20 link targets). The run continues, but the step is shown in red as "failed its bar" and counts against the idea.
- **redo**: retry with the failure as feedback; after 3 tries the block goes to ALERT.
- **kill**: the run stops until you confirm it (`gate kill`) or override it (`gate override "why"`).

Paid data comes from DataForSEO. The AI is Claude.

## Absolute bans

These are written into every `GUARDRAILS.md`:
- No pretending to be a government body.
- No "official" claims or OEM logos.
- No fake reviews, fake timers or fake scarcity.
- One honest account per platform.
- No invented numbers.
- No money moves without the owner.
- Caps everywhere.
- Thresholds are written before the test.

## Rules that keep the numbers honest

These apply across the blocks and come from an audit of a full run (2026-11-26).

- **Off-topic searches are thrown out (08).** After the real page-1 results are fetched, the AI reads the titles and the "People also ask" questions for every search. If Google shows something else for it (another meaning, a school programme, sellers instead of buyers), the search is removed and listed in `08_keywords_dropped.csv` with the reason. Intent labels are corrected from the same results.
- **A rival's bare brand name counts 10% (07, 12, 18).** Someone typing only a rival's name wants that rival's site. "X alternative", "X vs Y", "X review" and "X pricing" count in full. Learning searches count zero. Every report shows the split: commercial / brand / info.
- **Real rivals only (09).** Sites on page 1 are classified as direct, generalist or off-target. Off-target sites are removed (`11_competitors_removed.json`). The paying rivals found in block 02 are added even when they do not rank. Generalists never set the "weakest rival" number and their links are not used for link targets.
- **No data is UNKNOWN (03, report).** No trend data, no ads and no fresh discussions gives the status `unknown` and a warning, not "alive". After block 09 the 12-month trend of the real keywords fills the gap. The main score is shown only when step 17 is done; before that the report says "not yet".
- **Revenue proof (02, 04, 18).** A keyword count measures one channel. Block 02 also looks for real businesses in the niche with payment-verified revenue (TrustMRR, needs `TRUSTMRR_API_KEY` and one `vm proof sync`), records what they earn, charge and get per visitor, and counts the ads the top rivals run in the two public ad libraries (needs `VM_BROWSER_CMD`). It writes `02_proof.json`. Block 04 scores money and certainty from that proof, uses the best-earning rival's price, and may not mark an idea down for a missing or small search number. At decision 3 a niche where a close real business made `VM_PROOF_MIN_USD` ($5,000) or more in 30 days is never killed for being small in search: it gets a capped test of the channel that business uses. "Not checked" is always reported as not checked, never as "nothing found". `vm proof bands` prints what real sites earn per visitor by traffic size; `vm proof discover` lists young businesses that already earn.
- **Scoring (18).** The 7 checks (8 when the revenue proof was checked: the eighth, "do real businesses earn here?", is scored from the best comparable's payment-verified 30-day revenue, one step lower when that business is in a neighbouring niche) are combined with a geometric mean, so one very low score pulls the result down. Click price is averaged by search volume and capped when no advertiser was seen. "Easy to rank" is capped when there are few buying searches.
- **Market size rule (12, 18).** Two settings, both in money a month reachable through Google search: `VM_MIN_MARKET_USD_MONTH` ($1,000) and `VM_TARGET_MARKET_USD_MONTH` ($10,000). Under the smallest size: KILL as a search business, whatever the average says. Between the two: QUICK TEST at most. At the target or above: the score decides. The market score uses the same settings: under 0.3x the target = 2/10, 0.3x = 4/10, the target = 6/10, 2x = 8/10, 5x or more = 10/10. There are no sales goals anywhere. The market is restated at the price block 16 chose, and the formula text is rebuilt from the same numbers.
- **Link targets are filtered (13).** Strength 15 or more, at least 100 Google visits a month, and no casino, betting or spam-style names. Only the filtered list counts toward the 20 needed. Rejected sites go to `15_link_targets_rejected.csv`.
- **Overrides are written down (05, 14, 18).** A GO against the recommendation (weak first look, steps below their bar, or a KILL/TEST recommendation) is stored as `OVERRIDE: ...` and shown in every later report.
- **Plan rules (17).** One main search belongs to one page; extra pages on the same search become support pages with no target keyword. The home and money pages must target buying searches. A rival's own name is never rated easier than Hard and is left out of the "can a new site get onto Google" score. If the search market is under the size we want, the plan is test-first: the calendar starts after the validation test ends.
- **One offer (16).** The chosen offer is stored once (`offer.json`: model, price, currency, payment type, payer, channel, the one validation test). Block 16 may not move the idea to a different buyer or channel: a stronger option of that kind is set aside as "a different business" with a ready `vm new` line. Profit per hour is worked out at the visitors Google can really send, never more than 1,000.
- **Dates and forecast are worked out, not typed (17).** Dates start after the warm-up and the validation test. A money step ("$1,000 a month") gets a date only if the forecast reaches it inside 12 months; otherwise it says so. The good case is cut to 35% of the reachable market (`VM_GOOD_CASE_SHARE`).
- **Out-of-date steps (`vm/pipeline.py`).** Every block has a version and a list of the blocks it reads from. When a rule or a market setting changes, the blocks that used the old one and every block that reads from them are out of date: `vm run` re-runs them (cached data is not bought again; gates 1 and 2 are not asked again), the report carries an OUT OF DATE banner, and no decision except KILL is accepted.
- **Consistency checker (`vm/checks.py`, runs before gate 3).** One price in every part; the market number the same everywhere; forecast inside the reachable market; no calendar item before the test ends; money steps only where the forecast gets there; the same pages in the plan and the calendar; no thrown-out search as a page target; no euros, no sales-goal wording, no raw error text, no payment-type words that contradict the offer, no second test. Text problems are sent back to the AI up to 3 times; what is left is listed in the report as NOT CONSISTENT and GO / TEST are refused.
