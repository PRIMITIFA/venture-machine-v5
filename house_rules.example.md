# House rules (copy to house_rules.md and make them yours)

The AI checks every idea against these. An idea that breaks one is flagged in block 02.

## What I will build
- Digital products only, delivered 100% by software (no human doing the work for each order).
- Paid products, subscriptions or affiliate income. No ad-funded sites.
- Price between $10 and $1,000.
- Unpopular / boring niches are fine, even better.

## What I will not do
- No physical products, no dropshipping, no logistics.
- No sales calls; selling is self-serve checkout or email.
- No regulated advice (legal, medical, financial advice).

## My assets (for the synergy score)
- I can send cold email at volume (warmed inboxes).
- I build with an AI coding agent; MVP in 1-2 days of build time.
- About 2 hours a day to run each business.
